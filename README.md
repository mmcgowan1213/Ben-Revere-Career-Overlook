# Ben-Revere-Career-Overview

My favorite baseball player growing up was Ben Revere,
and it just so happened he was traded to my favorite team in 2013.

I built this project using R with the Lahman package and ggplot2 for visualization to look at key numbers throughout his career, 
like his AVG, OBP, SLG, and SB to showcase how unique of a player he was.
Since Lahman doesn't have all key stats, I found his AVG, OBP, and SLG by using mutate,
and the formulas to properly compute these numbers from that data that was available in Lahman.

Revere batted over .300 in his 2.5 years as a Phillie, racking up 95 stolen bases with the team.
He was acquired by the Blue Jays in mid-2015 to help aid their postseason run. 
He batted .318 in Toronto, but wasn't even given an offer by them in the following offseason.

Revere's elite speed and contact ability kept him employed for nearly a decade, 
but his historically low power numbers made him a liability that no team was willing to commit to long term.
